## II. PHÂN TÍCH CHI TIẾT TỪNG BÀI BÁO (PHƯƠNG PHÁP, KẾT QUẢ & GIỚI HẠN)

### 1. [L424] XGuardian: Towards Generalized, Explainable and More Effective Server-side Anti-cheat in First-Person Shooter Games
- **Tạp chí / Hội nghị**: `USENIX Security 2026` (2026)
- **Lĩnh vực**: Anti-cheat / cross-game generalization / explainability
- **Link Gốc / DOI**: [https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity26/presentation/zhang-jiayi](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity26/presentation/zhang-jiayi)
- **Phương pháp Đột phá (Key Method)**: USENIX Security 2026 system uses server-side pitch/yaw trajectory features and a GRU-CNN with SHAP-style explanation. Main CS2 data cover 3,069,216 aiming operations, 31,971 trajectories and 5,486 players; generalization is evaluated across CS2, CS:GO/HAWK data and Farlight84, with public datasets/artifacts.
- **Bằng chứng Thực nghiệm (Empirical Evidence)**: Cross-game generalization, large-scale real-world validation, explainability and some adversarial robustness in FPS anti-cheat are directly occupied.
- **Giới hạn & Điều kiện Biên (Limitations & Boundaries)**: Temporal calibration/false-positive drift after patches or legitimate meta/style changes is not the core evaluation.

### 2. [L415] Matchmaking Strategies for Maximizing Player Engagement in Video Games
- **Tạp chí / Hội nghị**: `Management Science 2026` (2026)
- **Lĩnh vực**: Operations research / matchmaking / bots / engagement
- **Link Gốc / DOI**: [https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2023.02957](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2023.02957)
- **Phương pháp Đột phá (Key Method)**: Management Science develops a dynamic matchmaking model with heterogeneous skill and loss aversion, considers bots/pay-to-win interventions, and evaluates the approach with real online-chess data.
- **Bằng chứng Thực nghiệm (Empirical Evidence)**: Mathematical optimization of matchmaking quality, waiting/engagement and bot interventions is mature enough to close generic live-service matchmaking theses.
- **Giới hạn & Điều kiện Biên (Limitations & Boundaries)**: Does not estimate a critical-mass viability surface across fragmented live-service segments, but combines with current production systems to make that an incremental/high-race extension.

### 3. [L393] Enhancing Automated Video Game Regression Testing through Behavior-Driven Development and Imitation Learning
- **Tạp chí / Hội nghị**: `GAS 2026 / IEEE-ACM` (2026)
- **Lĩnh vực**: Automated game regression testing / BDD / RL / imitation learning
- **Link Gốc / DOI**: [https://doi.org/10.1145/3786171.3788378](https://doi.org/10.1145/3786171.3788378)
- **Phương pháp Đột phá (Key Method)**: Combines natural-language BDD scenarios with imitation learning pretraining and RL fine-tuning to create adaptive automated regression tests; case study uses a Super Mario Bros clone in Godot and reports reduced test-development effort, improved coverage and detection of complex regressions.
- **Bằng chứng Thực nghiệm (Empirical Evidence)**: BDD specifications + learned gameplay agents for regression testing are already a current Games-and-Software-Engineering contribution.
- **Giới hạn & Điều kiện Biên (Limitations & Boundaries)**: Single case-study game; reward design and RL compute remain limitations. It does not define cross-version semantic equivalence of behavior or exploit mechanisms.

### 4. [L394] CausalGame: Benchmarking Causal Thinking of LLM Agents in Games
- **Tạp chí / Hội nghị**: `ICML 2026` (2026)
- **Lĩnh vực**: Causal reasoning / interactive games / agent diagnostics
- **Link Gốc / DOI**: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04293](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04293)
- **Phương pháp Đột phá (Key Method)**: ICML 2026 Oral benchmark with 14 SCM-governed interactive scenarios involving selection bias, measurement error and hidden confounding. Agents receive an experiment budget, design interventions and submit a final design plus explanation. Across 30 frontier LLM agents, the best reaches 68.0% survival versus analytical optima around 78–85%; only about 5–7% of sessions receive causal-reasoning credit. Behavioral failure analysis identifies component lock-in, bias and optimization drift.
- **Bằng chứng Thực nghiệm (Empirical Evidence)**: Interactive intervention design, hidden causal mechanism discovery, and judge-independent failure-mode diagnostics in games are directly occupied.
- **Giới hạn & Điều kiện Biên (Limitations & Boundaries)**: The games model scientific discovery rather than PCG or game-rule transfer; SCM structure is hidden but fixed. It does not test complexity-separating mechanic conjunctions.

### 5. [L135] Mortar: Evolving Mechanics for Automatic Game Design
- **Tạp chí / Hội nghị**: `GECCO 2026` (2026)
- **Lĩnh vực**: Automatic game design / quality diversity
- **Link Gốc / DOI**: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.00105](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.00105)
- **Phương pháp Đột phá (Key Method)**: Uses QD + LLM to evolve mechanics; evaluates their contribution to skill-based player ordering and synthesizes complete games.
- **Bằng chứng Thực nghiệm (Empirical Evidence)**: Mechanic generation plus skill-ordering evaluation is current 2026 prior art.
- **Giới hạn & Điều kiện Biên (Limitations & Boundaries)**: Still relies partly on user study and generated-game quality judgement; does not measure intrinsic computational demand or cross-rule transfer.

### 6. [L396] High Dimensional Procedural Content Generation
- **Tạp chí / Hội nghị**: `FDG 2026` (2026)
- **Lĩnh vực**: PCG / mechanics as coordinates / temporal-spatial representations
- **Link Gốc / DOI**: [https://doi.org/10.1145/3815598.3815606](https://doi.org/10.1145/3815598.3815606)
- **Phương pháp Đột phá (Key Method)**: Formalizes HDPCG by promoting non-geometric gameplay dimensions to explicit coordinates. Direction-Space validates reachability in (x,y,z,layer) for mechanics such as gravity inversion/parallel worlds; Direction-Time uses time-expanded graphs for dynamic action/conflict semantics. Multiple algorithms and Unity case studies evaluate playability, robustness, structure, style and efficiency.
- **Bằng chứng Thực nghiệm (Empirical Evidence)**: Mechanics-aware, temporal and higher-dimensional PCG representations are already an explicit FDG 2026 framework rather than an unoccupied direction.
- **Giới hạn & Điều kiện Biên (Limitations & Boundaries)**: The paper demonstrates selected mechanic dimensions rather than automatic representation selection, theorem-derived complexity activation or transfer laws across puzzle families.

### 7. [L397] Constraint-Based Four-Dimensional Spacetime Blending of Learned Game Mechanics Along Orthogonal Planes
- **Tạp chí / Hội nghị**: `FDG 2026` (2026)
- **Lĩnh vực**: Mechanic blending / learned mechanics / constraint PCG
- **Link Gốc / DOI**: [https://doi.org/10.1145/3815598.3815702](https://doi.org/10.1145/3815598.3815702)
- **Phương pháp Đột phá (Key Method)**: Learns tile-based mechanics from example playthroughs as changes in tile configurations, blends mechanics from separate 2D games along orthogonal planes to form a 3D game, and generates a solution jointly with the level using temporal constraints in a conceptual 4D spacetime representation.
- **Bằng chứng Thực nghiệm (Empirical Evidence)**: Learned cross-game mechanic blending with explicit compositional geometry and guaranteed-completion constraints is directly published in FDG 2026.
- **Giới hạn & Điều kiện Biên (Limitations & Boundaries)**: The work targets a specific orthogonal-plane blending construction rather than algebra-preserving learned generators or frozen transfer to unseen executable dynamics.

### 8. [L405] A Tractability Gap Beyond Nim-Sums: It's Hard to Tell Whether a Bunch of Superstars Are Losers
- **Tạp chí / Hội nghị**: `Theoretical Computer Science 2026` (2026)
- **Lĩnh vực**: Combinatorial games / composition / tractability gap
- **Link Gốc / DOI**: [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcs.2025.115656](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcs.2025.115656)
- **Phương pháp Đột phá (Key Method)**: Shows that disjunctive sums of simple tepid canonical game components (superstars) are NP-hard to solve, extending a long line where sums of individually tractable games can become PSPACE/NP-hard.
- **Bằng chứng Thực nghiệm (Empirical Evidence)**: The broad principle 'combining simple/tractable game components can create computational intractability' is already established beyond video-game mechanics.
- **Giới hạn & Điều kiện Biên (Limitations & Boundaries)**: Composition is algebraic/disjunctive sum, not semantic mechanic conjunction on a matched level; no finite empirical solver-activation study.

### 9. [L107] Representations for the Procedural Content Generation of Puzzle Game Instances: A Systematic Literature Review

### 12. [L353] Which Structural Constraints Are Learnable? A Regime Map for a Minecraft Voxel Generator
- **Tạp chí / Hội nghị**: `FDG 2026 PCG Workshop` (2026)
- **Lĩnh vực**: PCGML / controllability
- **Link Gốc / DOI**: [https://doi.org/10.1145/3815598.3815669](https://doi.org/10.1145/3815598.3815669)
- **Phương pháp Đột phá (Key Method)**: FDG 2026 diagnostic study fixes a VQ-VAE + autoregressive Transformer voxel pipeline and measures 14 structural properties under conditioning. Nine are controllable, four approachable and one unresponsive; a composite effective-signal × min(CV,1) predictor correlates with controllability at Spearman ρ=0.879 (permutation p=0.002, n=10 emergent properties). Authors explicitly warn this is one-pipeline association, not a universal predictor.
- **Bằng chứng Thực nghiệm (Empirical Evidence)**: Predicting whether structural PCG constraints are learnable/controllable and diagnosing frequency-floor vs representation-ceiling patterns is already a direct 2026 PCG object.
- **Giới hạn & Điều kiện Biên (Limitations & Boundaries)**: Single Minecraft voxel pipeline; bottleneck labels are heuristic rather than causally identified; predictor has not been externally validated across architectures/datasets/domains.

### 18. [L370] Beyond Fixed Tasks: Unsupervised Environment Design for Task-Level Pairs

### 19. [L193] Representing and Generating Levels Over Time through Playtrace Reconstructive Partitioning
- **Tạp chí / Hội nghị**: `FDG 2026` (2026)
- **Lĩnh vực**: Dynamic representation / PCG
- **Link Gốc / DOI**: [https://doi.org/10.1145/3815598.3815619](https://doi.org/10.1145/3815598.3815619)
- **Phương pháp Đột phá (Key Method)**: Full-text reread: defines the cake representation as a k×n×m Markovian sequence of game-state slices with persistent temporal entities, then introduces PRP, a time-aware reconstructive partitioning generator. Sokoban evaluation uses 12 human-authored 7×7 source levels and compares against PoD, Sturgeon-MKIII, Sturgeon-ST, two PCGRL settings, and an LSTM baseline. PRP reaches 100% playability and competitive diversity; case studies apply the same base algorithm to OvercookedAI and a new SkeleWalker domain.
- **Bằng chứng Thực nghiệm (Empirical Evidence)**: Dynamic temporal level representation plus domain-agnostic generation is already a full FDG 2026 contribution, including multi-domain case studies.
- **Giới hạn & Điều kiện Biên (Limitations & Boundaries)**: The authors explicitly note domain-specific pre/post-processing, potentially lossy conversion, non-identical baseline corpora, and lack of broad statistical comparison. They do NOT isolate the causal effect of representation from generator/search algorithm, and robust evaluation is primarily Sokoban.

### 21. [L246] Multiverse Mechanica: A Testbed for Learning Game Mechanics via Counterfactual Worlds
- **Tạp chí / Hội nghị**: `ICLR 2026` (2026)
- **Lĩnh vực**: Causal game mechanics / counterfactual worlds
- **Link Gốc / DOI**: [https://openreview.net/forum?id=5Q8r8ZubAH](https://openreview.net/forum?id=5Q8r8ZubAH)
- **Phương pháp Đột phá (Key Method)**: Full-paper audit: formalizes a mechanic as causal structure plus counterfactual constraints and generates 'consistent contrasts' from parallel game worlds with shared initial conditions/random seed so non-target context remains matched.
